# Tailor for a Job

When I share a job description, pasted or as a link (if you can't open the link, ask me to paste it):

1. Make a folder `output/applications/<company>-<role>/`, in lowercase with hyphens, like `finpay-backend-intern`. Save the job description there as `jd.md`, as given.
2. Read the job description. Sort out what they need most, what's nice to have, and the words they use for it.
3. Pick from my experience notes, not from my base resume. Something I left off the base resume might be the best match here.
4. Write `resume.md` in that folder, with the rules and format in [resume.md](resume.md). Lead with what this job cares about. Use their words where they truly describe what I did, since both screening software and recruiters look for them. Never claim a skill or experience I don't have.
5. Export it to PDF, following [export.md](export.md).
6. Tell me in a few sentences what you put first and why, and where I fall short of what they ask for. If I have experience that fills a gap, I'll tell you.

## Cover letter

When I ask for one, write `cover-letter.md` in the same folder and export it to PDF.

* About 250–350 words, one page.
* **Opening:** The role, and one specific reason I want this job at this company.
* **Middle:** One or two experiences from my notes that match what they need most, with specifics.
* **Close:** Short. Say I'd like to talk.
* Sound like me: plain and specific. No "I am writing to express my interest", and no praise for the company that anyone could write.
* Address the hiring manager by name if the job description gives one.

Why I want this company is the one thing you can't work out from my notes. If I haven't told you, ask me before writing.

Format:

```markdown
# Full Name

email · phone · City, Country · linkedin.com/in/you

Month D, YYYY

Hiring Team, Company

Dear Hiring Team,

One paragraph per block.

Best regards,
Full Name
```
