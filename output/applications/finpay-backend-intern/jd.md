# Backend Engineering Intern, FinPay

Bengaluru (hybrid) · Summer 2027 · 10–12 weeks

FinPay helps small businesses across India accept UPI and card payments and get paid the next day. Our backend team builds the payments and settlements services that move money for over 50,000 merchants.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain APIs for our payments and settlements services.
- Write tests and help keep our payment flows reliable.
- Look into slow queries and performance issues in PostgreSQL.
- Work with product and support to fix problems merchants run into.

## What we're looking for

- Studying Computer Science or a related field, graduating in 2027 or 2028.
- Comfortable with at least one backend language: Node.js, Python, Go, or Java.
- A good grasp of SQL and relational databases.
- You write tests without being asked.

## Nice to have

- Experience with payments or fintech products.
- Docker or cloud basics.
